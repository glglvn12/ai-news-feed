About this site

AI News Feed collects the latest AI research and release news from 29 sources into one page, so you can see in seconds what is new without visiting each site. Every item links to the original post; the short text under a headline is the source's own excerpt, never a summary written here.

What's on the page

  • Header: when the data was last fetched, how many items are new since your last visit, and which sources failed, if any. Refresh fetches every source again.
  • Major labs: the four newest posts from the major labs in the last 7 days. +N more shows the rest. Hidden while you search or filter.
  • The wire: every item, newest first, grouped by day. Search and the category and source filters narrow it.

Tags

Each item gets one tag, decided only by who published it.

  • MAJOR LAB The four frontier labs: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google (DeepMind, Gemini, Google Research) and Meta. News, research and publications.
  • BIG TECH Research from other big tech companies: Microsoft, Apple, Amazon, NVIDIA, IBM, Salesforce. NVIDIA and IBM are filtered to AI-related posts only.
  • LAB Independent AI companies: Mistral, xAI, Hugging Face, TypeSafe (Jev), Nous Research (Hermes).
  • PAPER Research papers and universities: arXiv cs.AI and cs.CL (papers about AI agents only, 30 newest per category), Hugging Face Daily Papers, Berkeley AI Research (BAIR).
  • PRESS Tech news: The Verge, TechCrunch, MIT Technology Review.
  • REPO AI-related repositories on GitHub Trending today.

Reading a row

  • The left column is the publish time in your time zone, or the age (11h, 2d) in the Major labs section.
  • — means the source gives a date but no time. est. means the source page has no date, so the item is dated the day it was first seen and listed last in that day.
  • A red dot marks an item that is new since your last visit. This is remembered by your browser only.
  • GitHub repositories are dated the day they first appeared on Trending.

How fresh is it

Items older than 60 days drop off. On your own computer, Refresh fetches live. When published online, the data updates once an hour and Refresh only reloads it.
